## 0.2.0

### Highlights

Rex 0.2 ships a full spec-driven runtime: streaming SSR through a render route, CSRF-protected form actions with schema-coerced FormData, static/SSG generation with stale-while-revalidate caching, and lazy-loaded page chunks under region error boundaries. `rex new` scaffolds DesignX-backed apps with shell overrides, Tailwind 4, i18n, and a lint generator, while `rex build --target` emits node/bun/deno/edge/static bundles with per-entry budget measurement. `rex check` catalogs 100+ RexError codes across boundary, naming, trap, token, a11y, media, and security rules, with `--runtime` mounting pages in happy-dom for parity testing. The demo is redesigned on DesignX with a collapsible sidebar, wallet balance card, sortable holdings table, and two-column send flow, gated by Core Web Vitals and Lighthouse E2E checks. CI includes typecheck, test, coverage, operability walk, audit, and license review with OIDC-provenanced releases.

- `rex new --ui designx` scaffolds a DesignX app with Shell.tsx, registry install, and Tailwind 4
- `rex build --target node|bun|deno|edge|static` with production bundles and size budgets
- `rex check` with 100+ catalogued error codes, a11y/token/security rules, and `--runtime` happy-dom parity
- Streaming SSR, CSRF-protected ActionForm, static/SSG pages, and stale-while-revalidate cache
- `rex/testing` helpers, Playwright operability walk, and DesignX demo with Web Vitals gate
